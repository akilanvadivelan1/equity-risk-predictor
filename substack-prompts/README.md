# Substack Writing Prompts

Writing prompts and outlines for Akilan Vadivelan's Substack posts on how companies
build, break, and reinvent business models.

Each topic lives in its own folder.

## Contents

- [`enron/`](./enron) — Finance used to deceive: the Enron story.
  - [`PROMPT.md`](./enron/PROMPT.md) — the writing prompt (voice, structure, concepts to teach).
  - [`OUTLINE.md`](./enron/OUTLINE.md) — a working outline that flags where to **research** vs. **understand**, plus a comprehension checklist.
